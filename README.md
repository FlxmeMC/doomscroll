# Doomscroll

**YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels, right inside Minecraft.** Watch, scroll, and like short-form videos in a clean HUD overlay without leaving the game.

## Features

- **YouTube Shorts & Instagram Reels.** Switch platforms anytime inside the editor (`]`) or with a keybind.
- **Frameless HUD & Mini Mode.** Keep a full 9:16 video on screen or shrink it to a compact card with `[`.
- **Drag & resize anywhere.** Press `]` to move the player or pull any edge to resize it.
- **Controls in chat.** Open chat to scrub, change volume, toggle subtitles, like, or scroll through videos.
- **Autoscroll & keybinds.** Automatically moves to the next video, with hotkeys for everything.
- **Comments viewer.** Read the top comments on any Short or Reel in a clean side panel. Toggle it with the comments button or `,`. View only, no posting.
- **Built-in sign in.** Connect your YouTube or Instagram account right inside the editor for your own feed and likes.
- **Zero FPS drop.** Runs locally in the background and pauses automatically when hidden.
