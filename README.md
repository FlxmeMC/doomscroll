# Doomscroll

**YouTube Shorts, right inside Minecraft.** Watch, scroll, and like Shorts in a clean HUD overlay without leaving the game.

## Features

- **Frameless HUD & Mini Mode.** Keep a full 9:16 Short on screen or shrink it to a compact card with **[**.
- **Drag & resize anywhere.** Press **]** to move the player or pull any edge to resize it.
- **Controls in chat.** Open chat to scrub, change volume, toggle subtitles, like, or scroll through Shorts.
- **Autoscroll & keybinds.** Automatically moves to the next Short, with hotkeys for everything.
- **Sign in optional.** Connect your YouTube account right inside the editor for your own feed and likes.
- **Zero FPS drop.** Runs locally in the background and pauses automatically when hidden.
